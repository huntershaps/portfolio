    Gallery 01

    The collections

    Each league keeps its own history. Choose one and every gallery below changes with it.

    Gallery 02

    The season archive

    One plaque per year, newest first. Pick a season to open its case file.

    Gallery 03

    Hall of Fame

    Inductees are grouped by the honour that got them in. New wings appear on their own as new honours are added.

    Gallery 04

    The trophy case

    Honours on one shelf, infamy on the other. Both count.

    Gallery 05

    Legendary moments

    The comebacks, the collapses, and the four-tenths of a point that people still bring up.

    Gallery 06

    Rivalries

    Head-to-head records, playoff meetings, and a rivalry score that is entirely subjective and entirely final.

    Gallery 07

    The record book

    High-water marks in one column, low-water marks in the other. Both are permanent.

    Gallery 08

    The memory wall

    Quotes, rule changes, and the things that do not fit in a table. Screenshots and images can live here too.
